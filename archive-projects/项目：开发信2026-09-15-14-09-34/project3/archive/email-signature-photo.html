Before you install — two things must happen first:
1. Your photo and the product banner must be uploaded to a public URL. An email signature cannot point at C:\Users\win\Desktop\pic.jpg — the recipient's mail server will never see it, and the image shows as a broken box. Upload both files (your headshot + z邮件.png) to your website's media folder or any image host, then replace the two src="..." paths in the code below with the live URLs.
2. Use a square crop. Your photo is 447×447 and already square. Circular crops work because of that. If you re-upload a different photo, crop it square first.
Design note: your photo has a near-black background (I measured it — brightness 3/255). I therefore built all three designs on a dark panel so the photo blends in seamlessly instead of showing a visible rectangle. That is a deliberate design choice, not an accident.
