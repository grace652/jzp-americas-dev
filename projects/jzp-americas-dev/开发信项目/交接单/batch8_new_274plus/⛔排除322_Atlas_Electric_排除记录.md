| 变压器品类页自称"OEM 供应与分销商" | *"Atlas Electric **stocks** hundreds of new, used, surplus and, reconditioned transformers. We are also **OEM suppliers and distributors** for several of the world's leading manufacturers…"* → 变压器本身**可能是转售**（其产品页列 Siemens 等**品牌**新余料）|
